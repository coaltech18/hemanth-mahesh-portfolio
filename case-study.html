01 / Context

Start with the human friction, not the feature list.

Good case studies do more than prove that Hemanth Mahesh can build software. They show why a product decision existed, which constraints shaped it and what had to become simpler for the user.

This template intentionally avoids the generic “problem / process / solution” portfolio rhythm. Each chapter focuses on a decision: what was understood, what changed because of that understanding and what engineering made the decision real.

02 / Product decision

Turn an abstract promise into a product rule.

If the promise is compatibility, the interface, data model and ranking logic all have to support that promise. The product cannot say one thing in the headline and behave like a generic listing directory underneath.

This is the section where Hemanth Mahesh can explain the trade-offs: what data mattered, what the system deliberately ignored, how trust was handled and which interaction reduced uncertainty for users.
